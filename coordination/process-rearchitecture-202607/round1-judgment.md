## E. Verdict

**JUDGMENT: MAJOR_CHANGES_REQUIRED**

The accepted challenges change the plan's safety posture (R9 split + live-proof gate — a mis-tiered live-runtime edit is exactly the class of incident this estate exists to prevent), its structure (single lane-policy home; tier-assignment audit; intake completeness over all terminal failure paths wired into existing validators; measurement spine; R14/R15 child plan), its sequencing (R7/R6 before R5/R8; ordered cross-repo subcommits), and its scope honesty (corrected uberarchitect evidence; conditional word target; +5 catalog cases; security drop made explicit) — that is MAJOR by the packet's own definition, and both adversaries independently reached the same verdict for largely non-overlapping, spot-check-confirmed reasons. None of it invalidates the wave architecture, the design principles (P1–P5 survive with P4 amended), or the operator-approved scope; plan v3 is a substantial revision, not a redesign, and round 2 (already budgeted) reviews v3.
